01 — About

Backend engineer, scale-first mindset

I'm a software engineer based in Gurugram, India, with a deep focus on Golang, micro-services and distributed systems — and, increasingly, the AI layer that sits on top of them. Most of my work lives on the unglamorous-but-critical side of the stack: services that have to stay fast and correct while handling enormous volumes of data.

Today at Expedia Group I'm building AI-integrated layers into backend systems — weaving LLMs, prompt engineering, AI skills and plugin integrations into services to solve problems traditional code couldn't. Before that, at CoinDCX, I built exchange-broker and blockchain-scanner services from scratch, rewrote a monolith into resilient micro-services, and load-tested infrastructure to survive 5× production traffic. Earlier, at Goibibo (GO-MMT), I built the streaming backend powering the app's home screen for millions of travellers.

I care about latency budgets, clean gRPC/Kafka contracts, solid testing, the CI/CD glue that makes shipping boring — and now, prompts and agents reliable enough to run in production.
